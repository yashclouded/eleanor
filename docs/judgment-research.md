# Judgment: content research and capacity

9 November 2026. Original authored draft problems; no runtime model calls, copied exercise statements, real personal data or live facts. This is a focused source review and mathematical design audit, not a systematic review or evidence that Eleanor improves real-world judgment.

## What the ten-year target means

One problem per five minutes continuously for ten Julian years is `10 × 365.25 × 24 × 60 / 5 = 1,051,920` problems. The acceptance target is a measured lower bound of **1,100,000 distinct visible Judgment prompts**. This deliberately assumes 24-hour solving; it is a capacity benchmark, not a recommended practice schedule or a claim that an easy question takes five minutes.

`npm run content:capacity:judgment` samples 50,000 reproducible seeds per audited advanced level, renders only each template's first context, and deduplicates the complete prompt globally using SHA-256. Foundation and original templates do not establish this lower bound: some legitimately contain read-off tasks or irrelevant distractors. The audit also excludes advanced selection-filter level 2, dependent-evidence level 2 and joint-bounds levels 1–2, whose independently varied ancillary givens do not affect the requested result. It counts neither answers nor unused parameters, item IDs, additional contexts, repair steps or solutions. The report in `build/review/judgment-capacity.json` states the observed lower bound, draws, exclusions, contributions and equivalent continuous years. Different numerical givens are variations of reasoning tasks, not millions of independent reasoning skills. Hash collisions could only reduce the reported count.

Finite procedural content is not literally infinite. Random delivery can repeat prompts long before the bank is exhausted. This expansion leaves ERM-1 selection, its 72-hour family cooldown, published-content filtering and placement rules unchanged. It does not promise duplicate-free delivery or uninterrupted practice. First-skin visible diversity is also checked per level by `content:check`, so hidden parameter variation no longer passes as visible variation.

Sensitivity is not the posterior probability; multiply likelihoods only when the required conditional independence is stated. |

| Risk framing | [Cochrane Handbook, chapter 15, §15.4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-15); [chapter 6, effect measures](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-06) | Separate percent change from percentage points, baseline risk from absolute benefit, and group-specific effects from population averages. | Independence | [MIT 6.041, lecture 3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-041-probabilistic-systems-analysis-and-applied-probability-fall-2010/b200b6217af1cd5dbea8c659ebbf046a_MIT6_041F10_L03.pdf) | Progress from independent repeated trials and complements to shared latent conditions and conditional dependence. Independence, conditional independence and mutual exclusivity are distinct. Supply a coherent joint distribution rather than asking the learner to infer independence from story wording. |
| Regression and selection | [Berkeley, errors in regression](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~stark/SticiGui/Text/regressionErrors.htm); [Berkeley STAT238, normal likelihoods](https://stat238.berkeley.edu/spring-2026/lectureeleven238spring2026/); [Penn State STAT504, three-way tables](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat504/Lesson05) | Separate an expected repeat outcome from an individual's guaranteed outcome. Harder tasks distinguish noisy selection, precision-weighted evidence and changes attributable to controls. A before/after improvement alone is not a causal treatment effect. | Odds and evidence | [MIT 6.041, conditional models and Bayes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-041-probabilistic-systems-analysis-and-applied-probability-fall-2010/b200b6217af1cd5dbea8c659ebbf046a_MIT6_041F10_L03.pdf); [Berkeley STAT238](https://stat238.berkeley.edu/spring-2026/lectureeleven238spring2026/) | Begin with probability-to-odds conversion, then likelihood updates, multiple hypotheses and conditional evidence. ## Progression and question quality

The existing 14 templates retain their identifiers and levels while constrained sampling is expanded. Each of the nine concepts gains a separate foundation ladder beginning at an estimated rating of 400–600 and a separate advanced ladder reaching at least 2600. New levels must change the requested inference or decision, rather than relabeling a larger-number calculation. Existing concept prerequisites and placement still control which levels a learner sees.

Draft ratings are author estimates of task demands. They are not measured problem difficulty and do not establish Olympiad or contest equivalence. Human review must inspect the progression, reading burden, realistic scales, arithmetic workload, answer tolerance, feedback and solution clarity. Calibration must confirm ordering before strong difficulty claims are made. More variants do not replace more representations or independently assessed transfer tasks.

For every template, the canonical solver is compared with an independent computation; sampled repairs finish at the same answer. Misconceptions represent particular wrong methods, not arbitrary offsets. Constraints avoid impossible probabilities, zero denominators and ambiguous optima. Small fixed fixtures exercise the central derivations in addition to seeded sampling. Historical instances are stored already rendered, so broadening these unpublished drafts does not rewrite previous prompts.

## Graphs and visual questions

[W3C's guidance on complex images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/) calls for text alternatives conveying essential graphical information. Charts therefore use labelled axes, units, a zero baseline, numeric data and a visible equivalent table. They render as local SVG with theme colours, no remote images or libraries, and no colour-only meaning. Bar charts compare given groups; line charts connect equally ordered categories or time points. Uneven numeric intervals must not be encoded with equal spacing as if they were a continuous numeric axis.

The chart title, data and descriptions must contain only givens. Do not label a series as correct, highlight an optimal choice, plot a computed posterior, or smuggle solutions into alternative text. Review previews include all source values as tables. The table provides an equivalent way to solve the task; visual acuity is not the skill being rated.

## Release and review

Follow-up: the [structural-variety expansion](judgment-variety.md) added 18 different task families on 10 November 2026. Judgment now has 50 templates and 207 levels, with choice, claim-selection and ranking tasks alongside numerical questions. The capacity figure below remains a conservative measurement of the earlier advanced subset.

Author validation on 9 November 2026: **32 Judgment templates, 145 levels**, including nine foundation ladders (400–1400) and nine advanced ladders (1800, 2100, 2400, 2700). The conservative capacity audit measured **1,597,763 distinct prompts in 1,600,000 draws**, equivalent to **15.19 continuous years** at five minutes each. The four exclusions leave 32 advanced levels in this count. This is evidence of available numerical variations, not 1.6 million different reasoning mechanisms.

`npm run check`, `npm test` (26 files, 239 tests), `npm run content:check` and `npm run build` passed. The content checker covers 2,000 seeds per level; the capacity audit counts rendered questions and does not independently verify every one of its 1.6 million draws. All 32 Judgment previews were generated. Five visual examples passed a local Chromium/axe check with zero automated WCAG A/AA findings and no page overflow at 390px. The production build still reports the existing large-chunk warning.

An independent code review identified and verified fixes for exact-boundary tank rounding, misconception statistics that did not match their feedback, a prematurely stated solution method, and capacity inflation from irrelevant givens. It also confirmed coherent models and no new blocking findings after those corrections. This agent review is an author check, not the required independent human review.

All added content remains `draft`, with reviewer and review date blank. Mathematical checks and capacity measurements are necessary author checks; they do not replace independent human wording and pedagogy review. `content/REVIEW.md` tracks every added template and links its generated preview. Publication is the existing human gate. No content is automatically synced or published by this work.
